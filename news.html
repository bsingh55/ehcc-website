6-a-side tournament

Our first club event of the season, on the Grand Final eve public holiday, with cricket, a BBQ and music all day.

  • 12 teams, $150 entry per team
  • 6 players per team: max two 1st XI players, at least one junior
  • Cash prize for the winning team

Senior players who want to be considered for selection are expected at club events like this one.
