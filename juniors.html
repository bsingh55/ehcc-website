For parents

New to junior cricket?

How do I register my child?

Junior registration is done online through PlayHQ. Register on PlayHQ. For Woolworths Cricket Blast (ages 5 to 10), contact the club and we'll send you the Blast registration link.

My child has never played. Where do they start?

Children aged 5 to 10 usually start with Woolworths Cricket Blast. Older beginners are welcome in our junior teams, and coaches will help them settle in.

Which team and day will my child play?

Teams are organised by age group across the FTGDCA (Friday evenings), DDCA (Saturday mornings) and ISEC (Sunday mornings). Our Junior Coordinator, Bhanu Singh, can help you find the right fit. Email endeavourhillscricketclub@gmail.com.

What should they bring?

A water bottle, a hat, sunscreen and comfortable sports shoes. Ask your team manager about anything else your child's team needs.

Is the club child safe?

Yes. We follow the Victorian Child Safe Standards, and all of our executive, coaches and team managers hold a Working with Children Check. Read more about child safety.

Can parents help out?

Please do. We always need team managers, scorers and helpers on game days. Let us know at endeavourhillscricketclub@gmail.com.
