Questions from parents

How do I register my child?

Junior teams register online through PlayHQ. For Cricket Blast, contact the club and we'll send you the link.

My child has never played. Where do they start?

Ages 5 to 10 usually start at Cricket Blast. Older beginners are welcome in our junior teams.

Which day will my child play?

It depends on age group and association. Our Junior Coordinator, Bhanu Singh, can help. Email endeavourhillscricketclub@gmail.com.

Is the club child safe?

Yes. We follow the Victorian Child Safe Standards, and our coaches and team managers hold a Working with Children Check. More about child safety
