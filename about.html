About us

One club. One team.

Fifty seasons of cricket at Sydney Pargeter Reserve.

Endeavour Hills Cricket Club has fielded teams for 50 seasons, growing from a single local side into a club that plays across seven competitions in Melbourne's south-east.

We run as one club across men's, junior, women's and veterans cricket. Senior players help coach and mentor the juniors, so a ten-year-old at Blast can see a clear path all the way to the 1st XI.

Over the years the Eagles have won 17 senior and 29 junior premierships.

Our home is Sydney Pargeter Reserve on Power Road, with five grounds, two turf wicket tables and turf practice wickets. Get directions

Child safety

We follow the Victorian Child Safe Standards. All of our executive, coaches and team managers hold a Working with Children Check.

Concerns can be raised with the Secretary on 0402 122 293 or at endeavourhillscricketclub@gmail.com. In an emergency, call 000.
