Our story

Fifty seasons of the Eagles

Endeavour Hills Cricket Club has fielded teams for 50 seasons, growing from a single local side into a club that plays across seven competitions in Melbourne's south-east.

We run as one integrated club across men's, junior, women's and veterans cricket. Senior players help with junior coaching, match-day preparation and mentoring, so a ten-year-old at Blast can see a clear path all the way to the 1st XI.

Over the years the Eagles have won 17 senior and 29 junior premierships across the WGCA, DDCA, MYCA, the All Girls competition and the VSDCA.

Family club

Parents play alongside their kids, and everyone from under 10s to over 70s shares the same clubrooms.

A clear pathway

From Woolworths Cricket Blast to junior teams, the J.G. Craig Shield and our VSDCA XIs.

Everyone welcome

Players from many backgrounds and every skill level. Come for a hit, stay for the community.

Child safety

Endeavour Hills Cricket Club follows the 11 Victorian Child Safe Standards. All of our executive, coaches and team managers hold a Working with Children Check.

If you have a concern about the safety of a child at the club, contact the Secretary on 0402 122 293 or email endeavourhillscricketclub@gmail.com. In an emergency, call 000. About the Child Safe Standards
