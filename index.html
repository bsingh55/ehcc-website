About the Eagles

One club. One team.

Endeavour Hills Cricket Club has fielded teams for 50 seasons, growing from a single local side into a club that plays across seven competitions in Melbourne's south-east.

We run as one integrated club across seniors, juniors, women's and veterans cricket. Senior players help with junior coaching, match-day preparation and mentoring, so a ten-year-old at Blast can see a clear path all the way to the 1st XI.

Over the years the Eagles have won 17 senior and 29 junior premierships across the WGCA, DDCA, MYCA, the All Girls competition and the VSDCA.

Our home is Sydney Pargeter Reserve, with five grounds, two turf tables and turf practice wickets, available to the club all year round.

Family club

Parents play alongside their kids, and everyone from under 10s to over 70s shares the same clubrooms.

Child safe

We follow the Victorian Child Safe Standards. All executives, coaches and team managers hold a Working with Children Check.

Everyone welcome

Players from many backgrounds and every skill level. Come for a hit, stay for the community.
