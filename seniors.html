VSDCA · 1st to 4th XI

Men's cricket

Turf cricket on Saturdays in the Victorian Sub-District Cricket Association, Victoria's second tier of club cricket, across all four grades.

Our seniors are part of one club with the juniors. Senior players help with coaching, match-day preparation and mentoring, and our J.G. Craig Shield program keeps strengthening the senior ranks.

Senior players who want to be considered for selection are expected at club functions and events.
